The 2-ton industrial mixture machine is manufactured for continuous production environments where consistent batch mixing is required. The heavy-duty mixing blades are designed for abrasive materials and can be replaced when worn.

**Drive System**

The machine uses an electric motor coupled to a helical gearbox, providing the torque required for dense, heavy materials. The drive is sized for the full 2-ton batch load.

**Mixing Action**

Counter-rotating blades or a planetary mixing action (depending on configuration) ensures thorough blending of dry and semi-dry materials. The mixing chamber geometry is designed to eliminate dead zones.

**Construction**

The drum and mixing chamber are fabricated from structural steel with wear-resistant lining on contact surfaces. The frame is designed for stability under full load and vibration during operation.
