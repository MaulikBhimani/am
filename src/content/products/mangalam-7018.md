Mangalam 7018 electrodes are suited to applications where low hydrogen content is critical — pressure vessels, structural members and high-restraint joints. The iron powder coating improves deposition efficiency and produces a flat, easily cleaned slag.

Store in a dry location. Re-dry at 300–350°C for 1 hour before use if exposed to moisture.
